# COC-KES-003 — Certificate of Conformance requirements — Kestrel Defense Systems

Each shipment to Kestrel Defense Systems must include a certification package containing the
following records, traceable to the heat lot of manufacture:

1. `chemistry-cert`
2. `mechanical-test`
3. `heat-treat-cert`
4. `dimensional-report`
5. `traceability-statement`
6. `radiographic-report`
7. `penetrant-report`
8. `first-article`

Missing records must be escalated to the quality manager before ship approval. Do not
substitute or summarize a record that is not on file.
