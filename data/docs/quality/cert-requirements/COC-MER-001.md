# COC-MER-001 — Certificate of Conformance requirements — Meridian Aerostructures

Each shipment to Meridian Aerostructures must include a certification package containing the
following records, traceable to the heat lot of manufacture:

1. `chemistry-cert`
2. `mechanical-test`
3. `traceability-statement`

Missing records must be escalated to the quality manager before ship approval. Do not
substitute or summarize a record that is not on file.
